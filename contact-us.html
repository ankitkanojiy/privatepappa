Offline Privacy Note

The PrivateVault Android apps request no Internet permission and use no developer-operated app server. The developer cannot access your vault, retrieve passwords or keys, reset a vault password, or recover encrypted files. Exported copies and backup files remain under the control of the destination you select.

Support Guidance

When asking for support, include your app version, Android version, device model, and a clear description of the issue. Do not send vault passwords or private files.
