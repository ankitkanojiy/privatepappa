1. Acceptance

By downloading, installing, or using PrivateVault or PrivateVault PRO, you agree to these Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the apps.

2. What the apps provide

PrivateVault and PrivateVault PRO are offline Android file vaults. They let you import selected content into app-private encrypted storage, unlock locally, create encrypted backups, restore supported backups, and use the management or viewing features available in your edition.

The Android apps request no Internet permission and do not provide a Privatepappa cloud account, cloud sync, remote password reset, or developer-operated recovery service.

Newly written/current-format vault files and metadata use versioned AES-256-GCM authenticated formats. New vault credentials and encrypted backups use PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations, unique random salts, and 256-bit derived keys. Supported legacy formats remain readable under their historical rules.

3. Edition availability

Both editions share the core encrypted-vault architecture. Features vary by edition and may change through future updates.

  • PrivateVault: essential encrypted import/export, password and optional biometric unlock, search and sorting, direct media capture, image viewing, activity logs, backup creation, and restore during initial vault setup.
  • PrivateVault PRO: additional organization, broader local viewers, Scan-to-PDF, Settings restore, higher-quality capture controls, vault safety checks, Data Control, optional 2FA Unlock, Panic Lock, and other advanced tools.

PRO-only viewer or management capabilities are not made available in Free merely because the shared code can recognize a format.

4. Passwords and authentication

You are responsible for choosing, remembering, and protecting vault and backup passwords. The developer does not possess your password or a universal recovery key and cannot decrypt a vault or backup for you.

Biometric Unlock is optional and depends on Android biometric and device-security configuration. It does not remove the need to keep the vault password safe. PrivateVault does not guarantee that every Android Keystore key is hardware-backed, StrongBox-protected, or immune to device compromise.

5. Backups and restore

You are responsible for creating, storing, and periodically testing independent backups of important data. New encrypted backups use the current PVBAK4 format; supported PVBAK1–3 backups remain restorable and are not automatically modified or deleted.

Restore uses authentication, integrity validation, app-private staging, and transactional activation. These controls reduce ambiguous partial state but cannot eliminate every risk from power loss, storage failure, operating-system termination, filesystem corruption, insufficient space, or damaged backup media.

6. Export, Share, and Open with

When you explicitly export, share, or open content in another app, decrypted data crosses the PrivateVault protection boundary. The destination app, storage provider, or recipient may retain, copy, synchronize, or disclose that content according to its own behavior and policies.

You are responsible for choosing trustworthy destinations and removing external copies when appropriate. Vault Reset and in-app deletion do not remove copies stored elsewhere.

7. Technical and security limitations

  • No software, encryption scheme, device, or storage medium can guarantee absolute protection in every situation.
  • Very large, malformed, corrupted, unusual, encrypted, or password-protected files may fail to open, render, import, export, back up, or restore.
  • Viewer fidelity varies by file format, structure, available memory, storage, Android version, and device capability. Office support is read-only and does not promise Microsoft Office parity.
  • Release builds request Android screenshot protection on vault screens, but device compromise, external cameras, and platform behavior remain outside the app’s control.
  • Deletion removes app-managed vault state but does not guarantee physical overwrite or forensic irrecoverability on flash storage.
  • Authenticated encryption can detect many modifications; it cannot recreate content that has already been lost.

You should protect your Android device, install trusted software, maintain adequate free storage, and keep verified backups.
