1. Scope

This policy describes PrivateVault, PrivateVault PRO, and the informational website at privatepappa.in. The Android apps are local encrypted file vaults. They do not depend on a Privatepappa account, cloud-sync service, remote key service, or developer-operated file server.

2. Data the Android apps do not collect

The Android apps do not send the following to the developer:

  • Files, folders, thumbnails, filenames, or metadata stored in your vault;
  • Vault passwords, backup passwords, derived keys, biometric templates, or encryption keys;
  • Encrypted local activity logs or optional failed-unlock camera captures;
  • Contacts, messages, call history, location, advertising identifiers, or behavioral profiles; or
  • Analytics, telemetry, advertising events, or remote crash reports.

The current Android manifests request neither INTERNET nor ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE.

3. Local data processing

When you choose a file or use a capture feature, the app processes that content locally to provide functions such as encryption, authenticated storage, thumbnails, search, supported viewing, backup, restore, export, and activity history.

Vault configuration, encrypted files, authenticated metadata, encrypted activity history, operation journals, and temporary staging data are stored in app-private device storage according to the feature being used. Process-local key material is used only for authorized operations and is not uploaded to the developer.

Optional biometric enrollment is password-first. Android performs biometric matching; PrivateVault does not receive or store fingerprint, face, or other biometric templates.

4. Android permissions

Permission availability varies by Android version. Examples of permissions used by features exposed in the installed edition include:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, and READ_MEDIA_AUDIO may be used to let you select supported media for local import.
  • Legacy READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE access may be requested through Android 12L/API 32; newer Android versions use scoped media access and system pickers.
  • Android’s system file and folder pickers may grant access only to locations or documents you select.
  • CAMERA supports direct photo/video capture, Scan-to-PDF where available, and optional PRO failed-unlock capture when enabled.
  • RECORD_AUDIO supports direct audio recording and audio captured with video, when enabled.
  • Biometric permissions allow Android biometric authentication; the app does not access biometric templates.
  • Notification and foreground-service permissions support visible progress for long-running vault operations such as restore.

Declining an optional permission may disable the related feature without changing the encryption of existing vault data.

5. Security controls

Newly written and migrated vault files use the PVL2 chunked AES-256-GCM format. Current-format vault metadata uses the PVMD v2 AES-256-GCM format with authenticated structural fields. New vault credentials use PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations, a unique random 32-byte salt, and a 256-bit derived key. Supported legacy vault data remains readable before migration.

New encrypted backups use PVBAK4 with a separate, fixed backup KDF profile using the same PBKDF2 algorithm, iteration count, salt length, and key length. Valid supported PVBAK1–3 backups remain restorable under their historical rules.

Optional Biometric Unlock uses Android BiometricPrompt and an authentication-bound Android Keystore key. The app does not claim that every active key is hardware-backed, StrongBox-protected, or attested.

No software or device can guarantee absolute security. Device compromise, malicious software, weak device authentication, storage failure, forgotten passwords, and unsafe exports remain relevant risks.

6. Exports, sharing, backups, and external apps

When you explicitly export, share, or choose Open with, PrivateVault may create a temporary decrypted app-private copy, grant the selected destination access, and schedule local cleanup. A receiving app or selected storage provider can retain its own copy. That copy is outside PrivateVault’s encryption boundary.

If a PRO user enables external Intrusion Detection copies, decrypted captures are saved to the selected external folder. Those optional copies are also outside vault encryption.

Encrypted backup files remain protected by their backup password and format while intact, but their storage location is selected and controlled through Android. PrivateVault does not automatically rewrite or delete older external backup files.

Links to Google Play, email, or websites open through Android or your browser. Those external services operate under their own privacy policies.

7. Retention, deletion, and reset

Vault data remains on the device until you delete it, reset the vault, uninstall the app, clear app data, or the operating system removes it. Delete actions remove the selected encrypted vault copy and its metadata reference through the app’s normal workflow. Vault Reset removes the app’s local vault state.

PrivateVault cannot guarantee physical overwrite or forensic irrecoverability on flash storage. Reset and deletion do not remove copies that you previously exported, shared, backed up, or stored elsewhere.

8. This website

This static website does not include an advertising, analytics, or tracking script. Its hosting and network providers may process ordinary request information, such as IP address, browser details, and timestamps, to deliver and protect the site under their own policies.

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to include and uses it to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, key material, private vault files, or sensitive personal content.

9. Children’s privacy

The apps and website are not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Android apps.

10. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when app capabilities, Android permissions, distribution requirements, or applicable laws change. The effective date at the top identifies the current published version.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, email reply@privatepappa.in. Please identify the app edition and version without including private vault data.
